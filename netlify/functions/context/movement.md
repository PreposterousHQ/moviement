# The Mental Health Reformation — Project Context

## What this is

The Mental Health Reformation Consortium (MHRC) is producing *The Unbreakable Spirit* — a Civil Rights MOVIEment for the Soul. A moviement is what a movement becomes when its primary carrier is story: film, performance, and participatory media.

This is not a wellness framework. It is not a clinical reform. It is a **civil rights framework** applied to the most intimate domain a civil right has ever been claimed for: the inner life.

For more than a century, psychiatry has held sole authority to define the human mind. The Civil Rights Moviement for the Soul returns that authority — respectfully, firmly, without apology — to the people whose minds are being defined.

## The rights claims

01. A person in crisis is *a relationship waiting to be understood*, not a malfunction to be managed.
02. Symptoms are, as often as not, **signals**.
03. Diagnosis is *not* identity.
04. Recovery is not the ceiling. Wholeness is.
05. The community is the medicine.
06. Authority over the definition of the human mind belongs to the people whose minds are being defined.

## The mind-map (Stone Synergy Soup)

The `/unbreakablespirit` app is the strategic mind-map of the production. The center node — the Stone Synergy Soup (SSS) — represents the whole interagency ecosystem. Surrounding it are episode holons, each tied to a co-star. The map has holonic structure: each node contains sub-holons, ingredients (contributing elements), and resonances (connections to other holons).

This is NJ AWARE's interagency strategic vision made visual and navigable.
